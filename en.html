Privacy Policy

Your photos remain under your control.

This policy explains how the GPS Damga Android app handles photos, location and technical data.

Effective: August 31, 2026 Package: com.gpsdamga.app v0.24.1 closed-test candidate

Summary

  • There is no user account or server operated by Albatros Development.
  • Photos, stamp data and the app catalog are processed on the device.
  • Location is optional and used only when you start a related feature.
  • Google Mobile Ads banners may appear on eligible content screens; when enabled on Google Play, an optional monthly auto-renewing Ad-free Use subscription is offered.
  • Google Maps, Places and Android location services may process data during online requests.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the GPS Damga Android app offered by Albatros Development. For privacy questions, contact albatros61development@gmail.com.

2. Data handled by the app

Photos and photo metadata

Photos you capture with the camera or select through the Android Photo Picker are processed on your device for preview, stamp creation, JPEG saving and the in-app gallery. The app may read EXIF fields such as date, time, orientation and location when present. A saved JPEG may include your chosen stamp information and coordinates when that setting is enabled.

Approximate and precise location

If you grant permission, approximate or precise device location is used to prepare an address and coordinate stamp, show a point on the map and optionally write location EXIF. Without location permission, you can still select photos, add date/time stamps and choose a map point manually.

Local catalog, drafts and settings

Folders, photo records, templates, display preferences, draft state and permission-request history may be stored in the app's local database or preferences. They are not synchronized to a server operated by Albatros Development.

Technical data handled by Google services

GPS Damga uses Google Play services Location, Maps SDK for Android, Places SDK for Android and Android Geocoder. When you use maps, address lookup or place search, Google or the geocoding provider on your device may process IP address, device and SDK metadata, SDK-specific pseudonymous identifiers, request parameters and usage information, crash diagnostics, map interactions, information about nearby Wi-Fi access points and, depending on how the service is invoked, location or search input to provide and improve its services. This processing is governed by the applicable provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising and consent data

GPS Damga uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show banners on eligible content screens and manage privacy choices. These services may transfer from your device to Google and process IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, Advertising ID, App Set ID and similar device or account identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Purchase and subscription information

When made available on Google Play, the optional monthly Ad-free Use subscription operates through Google Play Billing. Payment and card details are processed by Google Play; Albatros Development does not receive them. To start, verify, acknowledge and restore the subscription, the app temporarily receives and processes the product identifier, localized price, purchase and subscription status, acknowledgement information and purchase token from Google Play. The purchase token and price are not persistently stored; only a simple active/inactive record of the last verified ad-free entitlement is kept in private app preferences on the device.

3. Purposes

  • Capture or select, preview and create stamped JPEG photos;
  • show date, time, address and coordinates as chosen by the user;
  • provide map and place-search features;
  • preserve the local gallery, folders, templates and settings;
  • maintain basic app reliability when errors occur;
  • serve ads on eligible screens, measure ad performance and prevent fraud;
  • verify, acknowledge and restore the Ad-free Use subscription and disable ads while the entitlement is active.

Your GPS coordinates, entered address, photos and EXIF content are not added to ad requests or used for ad targeting. Google advertising services may infer approximate location from the IP address.

4. Local storage, retention and deletion

Stamped JPEG files are saved to the shared Pictures/GPS Damga area on your device. The app catalog, drafts, templates and settings may remain on the device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app.

Because generated JPEGs are stored in shared media storage, they may remain after the app is uninstalled. You can delete them through GPS Damga's deletion flow or Android gallery/file tools. Albatros Development cannot remotely access or delete these local files.

The last verified active ad-free entitlement may remain on the device until a Google Play query reports that the entitlement has ended, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The app does not receive card details; purchase tokens and prices are not persistently stored. Retention and deletion of data processed by Google are subject to Google's policies and user controls.

Android cloud backup is disabled for the app's data. Because the app has no account or backend operated by Albatros Development and your app content is not transferred to the developer, there is no separate developer-operated remote deletion method; local data is deleted on the device.

5. Sharing and third parties

GPS Damga does not automatically send your photos to other people. If you initiate the Android share sheet, the selected JPEG and its visible stamp/metadata are provided to the app or recipient you choose.

The following Google resources apply to map and place features:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps / Google Earth Additional Terms
  • Maps SDK data disclosure
  • Places SDK data disclosure
  • Google advertising technologies
  • How Google uses information from partner services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • Manage or cancel Google Play subscriptions

The app has no backend or proprietary analytics/crash-reporting provider operated by Albatros Development. Advertising and consent are handled through Google Mobile Ads and UMP.

7. Ads, subscription and analytics

GPS Damga may display one bottom adaptive Google Mobile Ads banner only in the main gallery view, an in-folder list or a real project list when that screen contains actual content. No ad request or reserved ad area is used in the camera, photo viewer, stamp editor, map, settings, maintenance, permission/form, error, loading or empty screens, or while a modal, drawer or selection state is active. The app does not use interstitial, app-open or mandatory video ads.

At each app launch, UMP checks the applicable privacy status. Depending on your region and the privacy messages published by Google, personalized, non-personalized or limited ads may be served. When Google requires the entry point, you can reopen your advertising choices through Settings > Privacy and permissions > Ad privacy options.

When made available on Google Play, GPS Damga offers an optional monthly Ad-free Use subscription. It renews automatically each month and only removes banner ads; core features remain available to free users. Google Play shows the localized price before purchase. You can manage or cancel the subscription through Google Play; uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel it. After cancellation, the ad-free entitlement may continue until the end of the paid period reported by Google Play.

The app uses no proprietary user-behavior analytics operated by Albatros Development. Processing performed by Google advertising services for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to minimize data, request only necessary permissions and keep photo processing and catalog operations on the device. Network traffic from Google Maps, Places, Mobile Ads and Play Billing is encrypted in transit. No device or transmission method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

9. Children's privacy

GPS Damga's Google Play target audience is 18 and over. The app does not knowingly request account or contact details from children and is not designed as a child-directed service.

10. Your rights and requests

You can review or withdraw permissions in Android settings and delete local photos and app data from your device. When UMP requires it, you can change advertising choices through Settings > Privacy and permissions > Ad privacy options, and use Advertising ID controls in Android's ad settings. For privacy questions or requests available under applicable law, contact albatros61development@gmail.com. Because on-device data is not transferred to us, deletion of that data must be performed on the device; Google's privacy controls apply to data processed by Google.

11. Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data-handling behavior or third-party services change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.
